🕉️ Book Vedic Poojas in Haridwar

Perform special Vedic rituals in Haridwar under the guidance of our experienced Tirth Purohits. All poojas are conducted at sacred ghats like Har Ki Pauri and nearby temples with complete samagri.

🌺 Narayani Pooja

A powerful ritual for protection, peace, and family harmony. It is performed to invoke the blessings of Goddess Durga and remove negative energies. Ideal for health, prosperity, and success.

🕉️ Tri Pindi Shradh

This shradh is performed to honor and liberate the last three generations of ancestors. It ensures peace to their souls and brings prosperity to the family by removing pitru dosha.

🔥 Hawan / Pujan

Performed for purification, vastu shanti, health, and spiritual cleansing. Suitable for home peace, birthdays, or starting any new venture. Mantras are chanted to purify the environment and self.

🕉️ Rudrabhishek

A sacred Abhishek of Lord Shiva with mantras and offerings like milk, honey, and bilva leaves. It is said to remove doshas, obstacles, and bring divine blessings.

👶 Mundan Sanskar

The child’s first haircut ritual performed with full Vedic procedure at Har Ki Pauri. It purifies and blesses the child with wisdom and long life.
