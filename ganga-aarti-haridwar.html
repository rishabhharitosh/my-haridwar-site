🔱 Book Ganga Aarti in Haridwar at Har Ki Pauri

Looking to book Ganga Aarti in Haridwar? At Har Ki Pauri, Ganga Aarti is a mesmerizing daily ritual performed with oil lamps, Vedic chanting, conch sounds, and deep spiritual vibes. The ritual is led by Tirth Purohits and conducted by Ganga Sabha.

The Ganga Aarti takes place twice every day — sunrise and sunset.
