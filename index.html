🧘‍♂️ Who is a Tirth Purohit?

A Tirth Purohit is a learned Brahmin priest who serves as the custodian of generations-old lineage records (Bahi Khata). They structurally guide devotees through sacred lifecycle and ancestral rituals across ancient pilgrimage destinations like Haridwar.

🔹 Pind Daan: For ancestors’ peace and ultimate salvation (Moksha).
🔹 Asthi Visarjan: Systematic ash immersion rituals in holy Ganga.
🔹 Shradh Ceremonies: Performing strict annual Vedic remembrance.
🔹 Genealogy Records: Verifying and updating ancestral logs.

"Ek Tirth Yatra bina Tirth Purohit ke adhoori hai."
